# 🎤 LexClarity - 3 Minute Demo Pitch Script

**[0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & The Problem**
"Hi Judges, we are excited to present **LexClarity**. 
We all know that legal documents are dense, confusing, and designed for lawyers, not for the everyday people who actually sign them. People are forced to either pay hundreds of dollars in legal fees just to understand a basic contract, or they blindly sign away their rights. 

The prompt challenged us to make legal assistance more accessible without crossing the boundary of offering formal legal advice. That’s exactly what LexClarity does."

**[0:30 - 1:15] Demo The Dashboard & Simplification (Screen Share: Dashboard -> Document View)**
"Let's look at a live example. Here is our platform. I'm going to upload a standard 'Software License Agreement'. 
Our backend immediately parses the document using **semantic chunking**.

Look at the **Split View**. On the left, we have the dense original legal text. On the right, LexClarity has translated it into plain English. But we went further—we identify the exact risk level of every clause. For example, the liability clause is marked 'High Risk' because it unfairly caps damages."

**[1:15 - 2:00] Demo The "Ask a Lawyer" & AI Chat (Screen Share: expanding a clause, opening chat)**
"Now, staying true to your 'Legal Boundary' rules: we don't replace lawyers, we prepare you for them. When a user clicks on a confusing clause, the UI dynamically generates **'Questions to Ask a Lawyer'**. This saves the user billable hours by letting them walk into a consultation fully prepared.

If the user still has questions, they can open our **AI Assistant Drawer**. If I type 'What happens if we cancel?', the AI dynamically searches the clause vectors, finds the termination rules, warns me about losing my data, and cites the exact section. No generic hallucinated answers."

**[2:00 - 2:45] Demo The Compare Tool (Screen Share: Compare View)**
"Finally, for our 'Innovation First' approach, we tackled contract negotiations. 
If someone sends you a modified contract, finding the tiny traps they slipped in is a nightmare. Our **Compare Tool** takes Version 1 and Version 2, and uses AI to generate a semantic diff. 

It doesn't just show words that changed; it generates an 'AI Impact Summary' at the top, telling the user things like: *'They doubled the liability cap, which limits your protection.'*"

**[2:45 - 3:00] Conclusion (Screen Share: Settings / Privacy)**
"LexClarity provides zero-retention privacy, instant comprehension, and safe boundaries. It democratizes access to basic legal understanding. Thank you!"
